What this page covers

Intelligence has always been a contest between hiding information and finding it: codes and codebreaking, signals and interception, messages concealed in plain sight. Quantum computing is the first technology in decades that could change the terms of that contest for everyone at once.

This page follows that contest through public developments: what changed, what it decides, and where informed people disagree. The disagreements are left open on purpose.
