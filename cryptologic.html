Purpose
Cryptologic Watch is my effort to understand, in depth, how cryptology and quantum technologies shape national security and intelligence strategy. News is the starting point, not the product. For each development, I trace the system underneath it: the science, the universities and companies building it, the programs funding it, the supply chains it depends on, and how governments are positioning around it. I'm a University of Illinois graduate from Chicago, pursuing a commission as a U.S. Navy Cryptologic Warfare Officer. This is an independent, AI-assisted learning project built only on public, unclassified sources. It does not represent the Navy or any government agency, does not assess the status of any specific U.S. system, and the views are my own.
Method

Intelligence has always been a contest between hiding information and finding it: codes and codebreaking, signals and interception, messages concealed in plain sight. Quantum computing is the first technology in decades that could change the terms of that contest for everyone at once.

This page follows that contest through public developments: what changed, what it decides, and where informed people disagree. The disagreements are left open on purpose.
